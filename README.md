## Real-World Experiments

### R1: Sounding-box selection

Two boxes were placed on the table, with one playing a beep. The robot had to identify and pick up the sounding box.

[▶ R1 video](assets/videos/r1.mp4)

### R2: Delayed-beep response

The robot had to remain stationary until a box began playing a beep and then pick it up. Sound onset was randomized between 3 and 12 seconds after each trial began. Any robot motion before sound onset or failure to initiate motion within 2 s afterward was counted as a failure, even if the box was subsequently retrieved.

[▶ R2 video](assets/videos/r2.mp4)

### R3: Bell-containing-box identification

Two boxes were initially placed on the table, one containing a bell. The robot had to grasp and shake the boxes to obtain auditory evidence, then place the bell-containing box in the receptacle. During deployment, we scaled the action commands used for shaking to increase the motion amplitude.

[▶ R3 video](assets/videos/r3.mp4)
